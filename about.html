Preserving Scholastic Tradition

Dedicated to the Art of Learning and Fine Stationery

Established over four decades ago, Stationery House began with a single vision: to ensure no scholar had to compromise on the purity, precision, or authenticity of their learning materials. What began as a humble storefront on the Kadapa bypass has blossomed into an institution trusted by thousands of families and academic trustees.

"We do not merely supply paper and ink. We construct the physical foundation upon which young minds ink their grandest questions and ideas."

Under our dedicated initiative, The Shelf — Curated Reads, we extend our passion beyond everyday curriculum textbooks into handpicked literature, historical chronicles, and artisan writing apparatus. Every notebook spine is structurally reinforced, every fountain nib tested for balanced flow, and every school curriculum pack checked against state board directives.

Chronicle of Growth

Four Decades of Academic Service

Trace the milestones that established Stationery House as a sanctuary for students, scholars, and educational institutions.

1984

The Foundational Desk

Opened our flagship doors in Kadapa with a modest shelf of certified school textbooks, drawing inks, and classic handmade ledger pads.

1998

Board Certification Alliance

Formalized direct procurement channels with national academic boards, becoming the primary regional distribution depot for verified school literature.

2012

Launch of Signature Ready-Kits

Introduced all-in-one grade packages, standardizing classroom supplies for over 200 partner schools across the region.

2026

The Shelf — Curated Reads Atelier

Unveiled our digital and archival curation branch, blending rare classics, specialized artisan writing implements, and rapid institutional fulfillment.
